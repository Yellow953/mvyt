| Fans Sued a Studio Because of Her | 0:41 | 62.3% | 0:21 | ~51% | ~0 |

- Both cleared the seed test (~1.2K views in the first 6–8h), then flatlined. The feed stopped expanding because both metrics sat under the breakout bar: VVSA 62% (<70%) and % viewed 35–51% (<65–70%).
- Bio/"rise" structure = "and then" storytelling. No open question means no reason to stay, no reason to comment, no reason to rewatch.
- 41–48s is too long for a cold audience. Target 22–30s, so the loop can push % viewed past 80–100%.
