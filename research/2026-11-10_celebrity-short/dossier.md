# Dossier: Ana de Armas (Short: "Fans sued over her")

## Who
- Ana de Armas, actress, born 1988 in Cuba (adult ✓). Spanish citizenship through her grandparents.

## Timeline (sourced)
- **At 18:** moved to Madrid with about €200. Reported amounts vary from €200 to €300 ([Yahoo/Bond interview](https://sg.news.yahoo.com/ana-de-armas-cuba-acting-160827374.html), [Biography](https://www.biography.com/actor/ana-de-armas)).
- **Spain:** got famous on the TV series *El Internado* ([Biography](https://www.biography.com/actor/ana-de-armas)).
- **US:** moved to LA without speaking English and took ~4 months of full-day English classes ([DMARGE](https://www.dmarge.com/ana-de-armas-english), [Boss Hunting](https://bosshunting.com.au/entertainment/movies/ana-de-armas-didnt-speak-english-war-dogs-knock-knock)).
- **Knock Knock (2015), her first American film:** "I learned it phonetically… I wasn't really sure what I was saying" (THR quote, via [Far Out](https://faroutmagazine.co.uk/role-ana-de-armas-had-to-learn-english-for/) and [THR Knives Out piece](https://www.hollywoodreporter.com/heat-vision/why-knives-star-ana-de-armas-declined-audition-at-first-1258369)).
- **Then:** Blade Runner 2049 (2017), Knives Out (2019), No Time to Die (2021).
- **Jan 2023:** Oscar nomination for Best Actress for *Blonde*, the first Cuban nominated in that category ([Olive Press](https://theolivepress.es/spain-news/2023/01/25/spanish-cuban-actress-ana-de-armas-bags-first-oscar-nomination-for-her-portrayal-of-the-original-blonde-bombshell-marilyn-monroe), [Biography](https://www.biography.com/actor/ana-de-armas)).
- **Yesterday lawsuit:** she appeared in the *Yesterday* (2019) trailer but was cut from the film. Two fans who each paid $3.99 to rent it sued Universal for $5M. In Dec 2022 Judge Stephen Wilson ruled that trailers can count as advertising under California law. The case was later thrown out ([Rolling Stone ruling](https://www.rollingstone.com/tv-movies/tv-movie-news/universal-studios-ana-de-armas-fans-false-advertising-court-ruling-1234651854/), [Rolling Stone dismissal](https://www.rollingstone.com/tv-movies/tv-movie-news/ana-de-armas-yesterday-lawsuit-universal-thrown-out-1234814836/)).
