# Dossier: Addison Rae (checked 2026-11-10)

## Who
Addison Rae Easterling, born **October 6, 2000** (adult, 26), raised in Lafayette, Louisiana. Source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Addison_Rae

## Timeline (all from Wikipedia unless noted)
- Dancing competitively since age 6.
- **July 2019**: joins TikTok. Within a few months she has 1M+ followers and leaves LSU in November 2019. Joins the Hype House in Dec 2019.
- **2019–2020**: Forbes' first TikTok earnings list puts her at #1 with about $5M pretax (June 2019 to June 2020). https://tubefilter.com/2020/08/06/addison-rae-tiktoks-highest-earning-star/
- **Mar 2021**: debut single "Obsessed". The project was shelved. She later called her early music "messy".
- **Aug 2021**: feature debut in Netflix's *He's All That*, the most-streamed film in its release week.
- **Mar 2024**: on Charli XCX's "Von Dutch" remix.
- **Aug 2024**: "Diet Pepsi" (Columbia), her first Billboard Hot 100 entry.
- **Jun 6, 2025**: album *Addison* debuts at #4 on the Billboard 200 (48.5K units).
- **Oct 2025**: Fallon performance of "Diet Pepsi" in Gypsy Rose Lee's 1950s gold gown.
- **Nov 2025**: Best New Artist nominee at the 68th Grammys, where she performed "Fame Is a Gun" in the nominees' medley.
- **May 2026**: 88M+ TikTok followers.
