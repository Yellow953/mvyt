# Packaging: titles, thumbnails, idea scoring

## Titles
- Keep them to 40–60 characters with the most interesting word first. Mostly sentence case, with at most 1–2 CAPS words for emphasis.
- One name, one stake. Use specific numbers.
- Formulas that outlie in our niche:
  - "The [adjective] Downfall of [Name]"
  - "How [Name] Went From [humble] to [peak]"
  - "[N] Influencers Who [shocking outcome]"
  - "Exposing the [thing] [Scam/Lie]"
  - "Why Everyone [verb] [Name]" / "The Truth About [Name]"
  - "[Name]'s [N] Craziest [moments]"
  - "This [Influencer/Model] Doesn't Exist"
- Never use a title the video doesn't pay off in the first sentence.
- Drop the hashtag soup in titles (the old channel did this). Put 2–3 tags in the description.

## Thumbnails (concepts for the edit stage)
- One face showing a strong emotion, or one striking object, plus 0–4 words of text that **add** to the title rather than repeat it.
- Make it legible at phone size: high contrast, a big face crop, a simple background.
- Use a "click-to-unpause" moment caught mid-action.
- Show contrast where possible: before/after, real/AI, $ amount.
- No sexualized crops of real people. That's a red line, and YouTube also limits reach on "racy" thumbnails.
- Always give 3 concepts so we can run YouTube A/B "Test & Compare".

## Idea scoring (for `ideas` mode, 1–5 each)
| Factor | 5 means |
|---|---|
| Proof | Research shows ≥10x outliers on this topic or format |
| Hookability | 3+ strong hooks across different archetypes come easily |
| Story | Clear conflict, a turn, and a payoff |
| Footage | Plenty of public clips and receipts |
| Timing | Trending now, or truly evergreen |
| Fit | A core pillar, nowhere near the red lines |
Total out of 30. **≥22 means script it next, 17–21 means backlog, ≤16 means drop.**
