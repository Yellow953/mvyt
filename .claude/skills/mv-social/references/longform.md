# Long-form retention playbook

Distilled from AgriciDaniel/claude-youtube `script` + retention guide (MIT), MrBeast's production memo principles, and teardowns of outliers in our niche (Internet Anarchist, RKH Media, CrimeChron style docs).

## Targets
- **Length 6–12 min.** Past 8 min unlocks mid-roll ads, but never pad. Padding costs more retention than mid-rolls earn.
- About 150 words per minute of VO. TTS reads faster, so check the duration after rendering the audio.
- Retention at 0:30 should be ≥70%. Most viewers leave in the first minute, so spend disproportionate effort there.

## Skeleton
| Section | When | Job |
|---|---|---|
| Hook | 0:00–0:30 | Grab, promise, stakes (see hooks.md). End on an open loop. |
| Context | 0:30–1:30 | Who they are and why we care, fast. Pay off the title's first promise *now*. |
| Block 1…N | the body | Each block: **reset line → content → receipt clip → micro-summary → forward hook** into the next block. |
| Mid CTA | ~25% | Soft, 1 line, tied to the content ("if you like internet lore like this, we do one every week"). |
| Re-hook | ~60% | A second hook. The 60% mark is a common drop-off cliff ("but everything so far? That was the warm-up"). |
| Climax | final 15–20% | The biggest reveal, the #1, the downfall moment. Save the best for last but tease it early. |
| Outro | last 20–30s | Final verdict or question for the comments, then an end-screen tease of the next video. No "thanks for watching". |

## Techniques
- **Open loops (suspension bridge):** open a question early and pay it off late. Keep 1–2 open at all times.
- **Rank order:** in countdowns, put the strongest items at #1 and #2 and the third strongest at #10 (the opener). Put the weaker ones in the middle, wrapped in interrupts.
- **But/therefore** links between beats, never "and then".
- **Receipts:** every claim gets a clip, screenshot or headline on screen within about 2 seconds.
- **Pattern interrupts** every 30–60s: a stat card, a zoom-punch, an SFX sting, a format shift (timeline graphic, map, side-by-side), or a rhetorical question to the viewer.
- **Visual cadence:** a new shot every 5–8s minimum. Use Ken Burns on stills and never hold a still longer than 4s.
- **Energy:** high for the first 3 minutes, then oscillate. Use a strategic pause (silence plus a black frame) before big reveals.
- **Comment bait:** a genuine question at the end ("which one was worse?"), never "comment below".

## Long-form checklist
- [ ] The first sentence echoes the title promise
- [ ] An open loop is set in the first 30s and paid off in the climax
- [ ] Every block ends with a forward hook
- [ ] The mid CTA is at about 25%, and the re-hook at about 60%
- [ ] The word count is within ±10% of minutes × 150
- [ ] At least 3 retention risks are marked with mitigations
- [ ] Every claim maps to a dossier entry, and allegations are attributed
- [ ] 3 titles, 3 thumbnail concepts, chapters, and a Shorts cut-list
