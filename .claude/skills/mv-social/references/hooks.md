# Hooks

Distilled from vyralcontent/content-skills (viral-hooks, MIT), AgriciDaniel/claude-youtube (MIT), and outlier teardowns. Adapted for ModelVault's niche.

## Three-layer hook (Shorts, and the first 5s of long-form)
All three layers must point at the same idea:
- **Visual:** the payoff or the conflict on frame 1, a "click-to-unpause" moment caught mid-action. No logo, no fade-in, no static face.
- **Verbal:** 5–10 words, landing before 1.5–2s. Lead with the stake or the payoff. Use power verbs ("torched", "cashed out", "got banned").
- **Text:** 3–7 words. It *sharpens* the VO rather than repeating it (VO: "She made two million dollars from one TikTok" → text: "$2M. ONE video."). Keep it in the safe zone.
- **Audio** (optional fourth layer): a bass hit, record scratch or sudden silence that locks the cut.

Test: muted, does the visual plus text still hook? Eyes closed, does the VO still hook?

## Archetypes (spread a batch across several)
| Archetype | Formula | ModelVault example |
|---|---|---|
| Investigator (reliable) | "I found the [hidden thing] behind [familiar thing]" | "The real reason every streamer invites Sky Bri" |
| Contrarian (highest ceiling) | "Everyone thinks X. It's actually Y." | "Andie Elle isn't the prank. The guys are." |
| Fortuneteller | "[Thing] is about to change [audience's world]" | "In two years your favorite IG model won't be human" |
| Downfall / stakes | "[Person] had [peak]. Then [fall]." | "She had 10M followers. Then one stream ended it." |
| Number | odd, specific number + payoff | "11 months. $2.3M. Zero face reveals." |
| Magician (visual-led) | "Look at this. That's actually [surprise]." | Show an IG model → "She doesn't exist." |
| In media res | Open on the peak moment of the story, then rewind | Drop into the meltdown clip → "3 weeks earlier…" |
| Direct call-out | "If you follow [X], you got played" | "If you follow any AI model, watch this" |
| Countdown | "[N] craziest [X], #1 is [tease category]" | "10 wildest Bop House moments. #1 got them evicted." |

Contrarian goes biggest when it lands, but the payoff must defend the claim. Investigator is the safe baseline.

## Long-form opener (0:00–0:30)
| Time | Job |
|---|---|
| 0:00–0:05 | **Grab:** the most dramatic clip or claim, mid-action. |
| 0:05–0:15 | **Promise:** exactly what they'll get ("…every controversy, ranked, and the one that actually ended her"). |
| 0:15–0:30 | **Stakes / open loop:** why it matters, plus a tease of the ending ("and the last one? Nobody saw it coming"). |
Target: ≥70% retention at 0:30.

## Anti-patterns (name them in critiques, then rewrite)
- **Welcome / logo / "today we're going to talk about…".** Cut it. Open on the payoff.
- **Vague tease** ("you won't believe…"). Name the *category* of the answer.
- **Bait-and-switch.** If you can't deliver it, change the hook.
- **Slow build.** Delete the first two sentences. Sentence three is usually the real hook.
- **Three-act hook.** The hook itself takes 8s. Cut it to one beat.
- **Subscribe-before-value.**
- **Imperative without earned payoff** ("stop scrolling").
- **Cleverness that needs decoding.** Clarity beats wit at second 1.
- **Single-layer hook.** A sharp line over a flat frame with no text.
