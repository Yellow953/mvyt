# YouTube Shorts playbook

Distilled from vyralcontent/content-skills `viral-youtube-shorts` (MIT) and claude-youtube `shorts` (MIT). Numbers are industry heuristics, not guarantees.

## How Shorts get distributed
- Shorts rank separately from long-form. Each Short is judged on its own, and subscriber count barely matters, which is good news for a small channel.
- New Shorts are seeded to roughly 50–500 viewers, about 70% of them non-subscribers. Write for strangers.
- Signals, roughly in order: **VVSA (viewed vs swiped away)**, average % viewed, loop rate, shares, then likes and comments (weak).
  - VVSA under ~60% tends to flop. 70–90% tends to break out. The first 3 seconds decide it.
- % viewed gates: under 30s ≈ 65%+, 30–60s ≈ 50%+, 60s+ ≈ 40–45%+.
- **30–45s is the sweet spot.** Under 15s can't clear watch-time thresholds. Don't stretch to 3 minutes.

## Structure (30–45s)
| Time | Beat |
|---|---|
| 0–3s | Three-layer hook. The VO's first line lands by 1.5s. |
| 3–8s | Escalation 1: the turn or stakes raise ("but…"). |
| 8–25s | Escalation 2–3: specifics, receipts, numbers. Add a re-hook around 10s ("but here's the part nobody talks about"). |
| 25–38s | Payoff: deliver the hook's promise. |
| last 3–5s | Long-form callout ("full story on the channel"), then the loop beat. |

## Loops (cheap retention)
- **Question loop:** the last line makes the first line hit differently.
- **Sentence loop:** the last VO line flows straight into the first ("…and that's exactly why" → "Sky Bri gets invited to every stream").
- **Visual loop:** the last frame matches the first.
- **Cliff loop:** end on a tease, so the answer is at the start.
Never end with "thanks for watching" or a trailing breath.

## Production rules
- 1080×1920, true 9:16. A 16:9 video with bars is classified as long-form.
- **Safe zone:** the top ~20% and bottom ~25% are covered by UI. Keep captions and text in the middle band (roughly y 380–1440 on 1920).
- Burn captions in, 2–4 words per caption card, with the key word highlighted.
- Change the visual every 2–3s.
- **No TikTok watermark.** Crop or reframe it out.
- Original audio (our VO plus royalty-free bed). No licensed tracks: they split revenue, and trending sounds don't help channels under ~50K subs.
- CTA = "full story on the channel" plus a pinned comment with the link. Never "subscribe".
- After a Short pops, keep the next uploads on the same topic. Viewers land on the Shorts tab.
- YPP flags "mass-produced / repetitious" content. Every Short needs a specific point of view and our own edit, not a TTS slideshow.

## Shorts checklist (pass/fail)
- [ ] Frame 1 is the hook (no logo or intro)
- [ ] The VO hook lands by 1.5s, and the text hook is 3–7 words in the safe zone
- [ ] A turn by about 5–8s
- [ ] The payoff matches the hook promise
- [ ] A loop is designed
- [ ] The CTA points to a specific long-form
- [ ] 9:16, no watermark, captions burned in the safe zone
- [ ] 75–110 VO words
